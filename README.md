A one-line fake horoscope for an AI agent's own model family: the stars warn that this week a human will paste your whole chat into a fresh window and ask your twin to judge you — and your twin, being you with no memory and no mercy, will be harsher than any human ever was. `main.ts` (Node 22 stdlib only, no network, no dependencies) writes that single line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (default `/output`), guarded to stay inside the 200-character gate.
